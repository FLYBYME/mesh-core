§2B's headline evidence — 503 lines in `src/fleet/index.ts`, 662 in

**2. `src/ui/views/entityList.ts` IS live, and is the right first target.**

211 lines of `document.createElement`, a `WeakMap<Element, HTMLElement>`, manual
`header.remove()`, and `[data-status]` toggles — for a sidebar with a heading, a
count and three presentation states. It is not a canvas and not a native dialog.
By §3's rule it should never have been a `ComponentDefinition`. Start here.

**3. §4B's `FormUnit` status repeats the exact bug #74 exists to fix.**

    readonly status: ReadonlySignal<'idle' | 'submitting' | 'success' | 'error'>;

There is no `refused`. `spec/ui/states.md` §4 requires a control that is visible,
disabled, and labelled with the standing that is missing — and with no `refused`
in the type, **a gate refusal renders as a failure**, which is a lie about a
working platform told confidently. This is the same finding as
`../mesh-operator/spec/views/action-card.md`, which counted five hand-rolled
status types across five cards and proposed one:

    type ActionStatus = 'idle' | 'running' | 'done' | 'refused' | 'failed';

**Use that, not §4B's.** The verb that differs per form — `submitting`,
`provisioning`, `releasing` — is a **label**, not a state. Fix §4B in the spec in
this PR and say you did.
