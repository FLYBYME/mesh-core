================================================================================
WHAT TO WATCH
================================================================================

**A prop cannot be a callback.** `Props` is `Reactive<Json> | undefined`, so a
component takes values and getters, and **per-item rendering belongs to the
caller** — `ui.EntityList` owns the frame and the status presentation, and
`catalog` supplies the rows as children via `each()`. If a component seems to
need a callback, it needs a different shape, not a widened `Props`.

**Do not extract shared style constants and do not add a `styles.ts`.** Styling
that is still needed after adoption stays inline for now; a helper shared between
parts couples two independently versioned artifacts.

**Do not touch `releases`.** It is the second console and it is the next dispatch
— keeping it untouched is what makes this one reviewable.

**Do not adopt the internal context** (kernel 0.14's separation of a view's state
from the published API). Separate task.

Foreground commands, commit as you go.
