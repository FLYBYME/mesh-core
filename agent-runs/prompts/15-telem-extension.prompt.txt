`TelemEventInputSchema` is `.strict()`. One unknown field rejects the whole
batch, so guessing a field name loses 100 events silently. Match it exactly.

THE HARD PART IS NOT THE POST

It is that this extension must be incapable of harming a page. Specifically:

  * activate must not throw, ever, whatever it finds
  * it must not block boot — no awaiting a POST during activation
  * a failing endpoint must degrade to dropping events, not to retrying forever
    or growing a buffer without bound
  * the last batch has to survive the tab closing: flush on `visibilitychange`,
    and `navigator.sendBeacon` where available, because a `fetch` in `unload` is
    cancelled

Write a test for each of those. They are the reason this is a careful piece of
work rather than a small one, and they are the paths nobody writes tests for.

WHAT IT COLLECTS, AND WHERE IT IS ALREADY AVAILABLE

  * logs — mesh-web's `createLogBuffer` already holds them and nothing reads it
  * calls — the client reports outcome and duration; do not wrap `fetch`
  * boot — which parts mounted, in what order, which failed
  * errors — `window.onerror` and `unhandledrejection`

contents. The server's schema is shaped to make that easy; do not route around
