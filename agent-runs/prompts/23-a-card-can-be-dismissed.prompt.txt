--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — U5: `ActionCard` can be submitted and cannot be dismissed

`ActionCardProps` has `primaryLabel`, `confirmation`, `onResult` and `onError`,
and **no secondary action**. A card opened in place has no way out of itself, so
every screen that opens one writes its own Cancel beside it — the wheel `ui`
exists to stop being reinvented, in the component whose whole job is to be the
form nobody hand-writes.

`src/identity/view.ts` does exactly that today: it wraps the card in a `Stack`
with its own button, and says so at the call site. The second screen to open a
form will write a slightly different one.

**Wanted:** `secondaryLabel` and `onSecondary`, defaulting to absent so a card
that is not dismissible stays exactly as it is. Then delete the hand-written
Cancel in `identity/view.ts` and the comment apologising for it.

**Test it as behaviour.** Press the secondary control and assert the card
dismissed and **the primary action did not run** — a Cancel that also submits is
the failure worth catching, and asserting a label exists catches nothing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — THE DECISION U5 NAMES: IN PLACE, OR OVER

Worth deciding at the same time and **not the same thing**:

    `confirm` is already a layer that dims and covers, and `spec/ui/anatomy.md`
    gives an app regions rather than layers. The identity form is a band that
    pushes the regions down, on the argument that nothing it covers is needed to
    fill it in -- but that is one screen's judgement, made once, and it is the
    kind of thing the vocabulary should decide for everybody.

Decide it, write it into `spec/ui/` where a screen author will find it, and make
`ActionCard` obey whichever you chose. If the answer is *it depends*, then say
what it depends on **as a prop with two values**, not as a paragraph — a rule a
component cannot express is a rule that will be broken, which is this track's
own sentence.

Note U6 before you choose: a `Dialog` opened at mount is **not modal** — the
element is not connected, `showModal()` throws, and mesh-web's fallback leaves it
`position: absolute` over the window's content. So *over* is not currently
available at mount even if you want it. That is mesh-web's bug and not yours to
fix; it is a constraint on what you can ship today, and the roadmap entry should
say which of the two you would have chosen without it.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — THE IDENTITY APP ANSWERS ALL FIVE STATES

`spec/ui/states.md`: *"Every view answers all five. Not 'handles' — **answers**,
with something drawn."* The five are loading, empty, unauthenticated, refused,
error.

Two of them only became expressible today, so no app has used them yet:

- **refused, arriving.** `ui.EntityList` and `ui.Table` now take
  `error: string | { refused: string }`. A read that fails with 403 is mapped to
  `{ refused: reason }` and renders states §4 rather than §5. U2.
- **refused, foreseen, and busy.** `ui.ActionButton` reads `command.available()`
  and renders disabled with the reason; it cannot be fired twice. U3.

Go through `src/identity/` and make every view answer all five, using those
rather than hand-rolled strings. `states.md` §4 gives the model to copy — three
refusals, three different sentences, each naming what would fix it:

    if (a.why === 'needs_operator') return 'Platform operator standing required ...';
    if (a.why === 'needs_session')  return 'Sign in as an operator to ...';
    if (a.why === 'not_exposed')    return 'identity.grant_role is not exposed on this server.';

**Never invent a friendlier message.** The platform's errors are good and
replacing one with *Something went wrong* destroys the only useful thing in the
response.

**A conformance test, not a promise.** `spec/apps/README.md` §6 says an app spec
lists what each of the five looks like and the conformance test fails a view that
cannot produce all five. Write that test for identity: drive the app into each of
the five and assert something specific was drawn. If a state cannot be reached,
that is the finding — write it into the roadmap rather than skipping the case.

WHAT NOT TO DO

- Do not touch `@flybyme/mesh` (frozen) or `@flybyme/mesh-web` (separate
  repository, separate dispatch may be running). If you need something from the
  kernel that does not exist, write it into `spec/roadmap.md` as a mesh-web item
  with its evidence and work around it here.
- Do not edit mesh-operator or mesh-serve.
- `as any` and `as never` are bugs here, not style nits. A dispatch in another
  repository shipped four `as any` in one test today and they were taken back out
  within the hour. If you need one you have the wrong shape — say so in the
  report.
- Do not redesign the token set or the states type. Both landed today; spend
  them, do not revise them.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
COMMIT AS YOU GO — NOT ADVICE

A dispatch was killed mid-run today and left eight modified files uncommitted in
its worktree; the work survived only because a person went and looked. **A commit
survives the process ending; an unstaged edit does not.** Never background a
command: run `npm test` in the foreground and wait.
