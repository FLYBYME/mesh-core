================================================================================
WHAT THIS IS NOT
================================================================================

**Not a styling fix.** `console.ts` carries 74 `style: {}` objects and 188
style-property lines — 21% of the file. That is real and it is **not this task**:
it needs the `ui.*` component vocabulary (`mesh-web/spec/components.md`), which
does not exist yet. Do not invent a local component layer, do not add a
`styles.ts`, do not extract shared style constants — a helper shared between two
parts couples two independently versioned artifacts, which is the hazard
`boundaries.md` names. Leave the styling where it is and let it move once.

**Not a behaviour change.** `console.localhost` is live on both parts right now.
The diff must be extraction and composition, nothing else. No renamed exports, no
changed props, no fixes in passing — find a bug, report it, leave it.

**Not a rewrite of what dispatch 4 did.** Both consoles were moved onto the
`models` capability. Do not adjust that.

    node -e "console.log(require('./node_modules/@flybyme/mesh-web/package.json').version)"

it is not 0.13.0: `rm -rf node_modules/@flybyme/mesh-web && npm install`.
